SHORT PRIVACY NOTICE (ENGLISH)

Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash/technical error reports, stack traces, device/app versions, limited screen/action codes, map load time/stage, connection type (Wi‑Fi/cellular/VPN), internet validation/restriction, Google Play services status, and Maps/Places/Photon failure types. Response codes are included only when provided by the service. API key values/hashes, SSID, IP address, search text, reminder content, place names, coordinates, and location history are not added. Reports are linked to an installation identifier; reporting can be turned off in Settings.

The app uses precise and background location, with your permission, to detect when you reach reminder areas you choose. It does not create a route or location history on the developer's own servers. Reminder titles, notes, chosen place names and coordinates, radius, time window, repeat schedule, and status are stored on your device by default. If you sign in with Google and separately enable backup, reminder details are stored in your Firebase account. Google sign-in processes account identifiers such as name, email address, and Firebase user ID.

Location and related requests may be sent to Google Maps/Places for map and place-search features, Android/Google Play services for geofence detection, and Open-Meteo for local weather. Date and language may be sent to Wikimedia for the “this day in history” card. If you enable app announcements, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a technical notification token. Google Play processes subscription purchases; the developer does not receive payment card details. When ads are enabled in Standard, Google AdMob may process IP address, app/device identifiers, and ad interactions under its policies. The app does not add reminder GPS coordinates to ad requests. Plus does not show ads. If Google Places search fails, address or business queries may be sent to Photon (Komoot) as a fallback, and selected coordinates may be sent for reverse geocoding. Photon results use OpenStreetMap data.

On-device reminders remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. The Settings > Delete my account option, after reauthentication, deletes the Firebase account and its reminder backups. Third-party providers may retain technical logs under their own policies. For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the developer at the email above.
