SHORT PRIVACY NOTICE (ENGLISH)

The app uses precise and background location, with your permission, to detect when you reach reminder areas you choose. It does not create a route or location history on the developer's own servers. Reminder titles, notes, chosen place names and coordinates, radius, time window, repeat schedule, and status are stored on your device by default. If you sign in with Google and separately enable backup, reminder details are stored in your Firebase account. Google sign-in processes account identifiers such as name, email address, and Firebase user ID.

Location and related requests may be sent to Google Maps/Places for map and place-search features, Android/Google Play services for geofence detection, and Open-Meteo for local weather. Date and language may be sent to Wikimedia for the “this day in history” card. If you enable app announcements, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a technical notification token. Google Play processes subscription purchases; the developer does not receive payment card details. When ads are enabled in Standard, Google AdMob may process IP address, app/device identifiers, and ad interactions under its policies. The app does not add reminder GPS coordinates to ad requests. Plus does not show ads.

On-device reminders remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. The Settings > Delete my account option, after reauthentication, deletes the Firebase account and its reminder backups. Third-party providers may retain technical logs under their own policies. For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the developer at the email above.

AVISO DE PRIVACIDAD (ESPAÑOL)
La app usa la ubicación precisa y en segundo plano, con tu permiso, para detectar tu llegada a zonas de recordatorio elegidas. No crea un historial de recorridos en servidores propios. Los recordatorios se guardan en el dispositivo, salvo que inicies sesión con Google y actives la copia en Firebase. La app no añade coordenadas GPS precisas de los recordatorios a solicitudes publicitarias. Puedes eliminar los datos locales desde la app y borrar la cuenta y las copias Firebase en Ajustes > Eliminar mi cuenta. Contacto: contactwithogh@gmail.com.
